### Matchup
- Definition：A head-to-head comparison between two specific decks, simulating multiple games to determine win rates and statistical advantages.
- Aliases：matchup

### Win Rate
- Definition：The percentage of games won by a deck against another deck in simulated matchups, calculated as (wins / total_matches) * 100.
- Aliases：win rate、winrate

### Deck A vs Deck B
- Definition：The standard format for specifying two competing decks in a simulation, where Deck A plays first and Deck B goes second.
- Aliases：deck-a、deck-b

### All Matchups Mode
- Definition：A simulation mode that runs every possible pairing between all available decks to generate a comprehensive ranking.
- Aliases：all-matchups

### State-Based Actions (SBAs)
- Definition：Automatic game state checks that occur during gameplay, such as creature destruction when toughness reaches 0 or player death at 0 life.
- Aliases：SBA、state-based actions

### Stack Resolution
- Definition：The process of resolving spells and abilities in reverse order, following Magic's priority system and spell resolution rules.
- Aliases：stack、spell resolution

### Mana Pool
- Definition：Temporary storage of mana produced from lands that can be used to pay spell costs, cleared at the end of each turn.
- Aliases：mana pool、pool

### Keywords
- Definition：Special abilities like Flying, Trample, Lifelink, Haste, etc., that modify how creatures and spells behave according to Magic rules.
- Aliases：keyword、keywords

### Zones
- Definition：Game areas including Library, Hand, Battlefield, Graveyard, Exile, and Stack where cards can exist during a match.
- Aliases：zone、zones

### Turn Structure
- Definition：The phases of a turn: Beginning (untap, upkeep, draw), Main Phase (pre-combat), Combat Phase, Main Phase (post-combat), and Ending (end, cleanup).
- Aliases：turn、phases
